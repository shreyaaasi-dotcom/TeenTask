WHAT IS TEENTASK?

Local opportunities

TeenTask is meant for teenagers to discover opportunities to earn money whilst helping families and neighbors. Whether it's babysitting, assisting seniors, or taking care of backyard tasks, there's always a place to put your skills to use! People interested in getting an extra hand are encouraged to post their job offers here as well.

How TeenTask works

Explore

Create an account based on whether you are looking for a job or want one done. Remember to edit your profile.

Connect

Find local job offers and accept them to build your portfolio and experience!.

Earn

Build experience and earn while contributing to your community.
